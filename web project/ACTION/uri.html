STORY LINE

A decorated Indian officer returns from a draining counter insurgency tour, only to face a brutal catalyst when militants assault an army installation. Rage spreads through the ranks. Political leaders demand a response that hits harder than words. He joins a covert unit tasked with planning a precise retaliation across the border. Preparation becomes obsession. Surveillance, prototypes, rehearsals. Pressure mounts as each delay sharpens the need for retribution. The mission demands stealth and nerve. Yet the officer's defining vice is pure fury. If he loses control for even a second, the entire operation collapses and the consequences ripple far beyond the battlefield.
