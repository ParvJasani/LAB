Plot Synopsis

Salaar (2017) follows Deva and Vardha, childhood friends from Khansaar. Years after Deva leaves Khansaar, Vardha calls him back to protect him during a struggle for power.
Aadhya, an NRI woman, becomes a target of Khansaar's political factions and is hidden with Deva in Assam. When attackers threaten her, Deva's fighting skills reveal his connection to Khansaar. Vardha then summons Deva to Khansaar. Khansaar was founded by the Mannar, Shouryaanga, and Ghaniyaar tribes. Raja Mannar later massacred the Shouryaangas to strengthen his rule. However, some survived, including Deva, who is secretly the son of Dhaara Raisaar, the rightful Shouryaanga heir. When Vardha returns to Khansaar, a power struggle begins between the Mannars, Doras, and rival factions. Deva repeatedly protects Vardha, even killing those who threaten him. Eventually, Vardha learns Deva's true identity and publicly declares him his Salaar, accepting him as his trusted warrior and rightful heir.
